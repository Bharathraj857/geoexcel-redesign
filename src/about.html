About us

Geographic Excellence, built on four decades of work

With a heritage dating back to the 1980s, the name "GeoExcel", short for Geographic Excellence, was formally adopted in 2023.

Who we are

From growing rice, sugarcane and sesame, and a single cement and steel dealership, the GeoExcel Group has grown into a business with multiple verticals spanning agriculture, automotive, construction and trading.

Geo Excel is the group's trading company. Based in Bangalore, it facilitates the movement of goods between countries and markets through import and export, covering the production, procuring, packing and distribution of agricultural and food products.

Our stated clients include food grain traders, food processing companies and government agencies.

Group verticals

  • Agriculture
  • Automotive
  • Construction
  • Trading

Our journey

  1. 1980sAgriculture. Started as farmers growing and harvesting rice, sugarcane and sesame.
  2. 1990sCement & steel dealers. Diversification into cement and steel gave a strong foundation for civil contracting services.
  3. 1993–2014Civil construction. Turnkey residential construction in Bangalore from 1993, expanding by 2014 into commercial, industrial and infrastructure work, with some own developments under the name "Anuadi Constructions".
  4. 2019Sustainability & CSR. A focus on the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals and passion-driven CSR activities.
  5. 2023Birth of Geo Excel. Engaging in the export trade of the products in our catalogue.

What we do

Trading and supply-chain solutions for agricultural and food products.
