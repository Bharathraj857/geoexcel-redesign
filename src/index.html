About Geo Excel

Geographic Excellence, rooted in four decades of work

The name "GeoExcel", short for Geographic Excellence, was formally adopted in 2023, building on a heritage that dates back to the 1980s. From growing rice, sugarcane and sesame, and a single cement and steel dealership, the GeoExcel Group now spans agriculture, automotive, construction and trading.

Read our story
  1. 1980sAgriculture. Growing and harvesting rice, sugarcane and sesame.
  2. 1990sCement & steel dealers. A foundation for civil contracting.
  3. 1993–2014Civil construction in Bangalore, from residential to infrastructure.
  4. 2023Geo Excel is formed for the export trade of the products in our catalogue.
