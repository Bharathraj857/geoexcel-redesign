Sustainability

Building an environmentally sustainable business

Since 2019 the GeoExcel Group has focused its CSR activities on the United Nations' 17 Sustainable Development Goals.

Our approach

  • Uplifting livelihoods and ensuring good working conditions
  • Implementing living wages for all stakeholders
  • Acting on climate change
  • Building an environmentally sustainable business

Principles we follow

  • We follow sustainable agricultural methods for collecting natural raw materials.
  • We source natural raw materials using proper harvesting methods, at the right time and for the right price.
  • We support the preservation of endangered plant species and avoid using them.
  • We practise organic farming processes and avoid pesticides and chemicals.
  • We avoid raw materials that may affect climate conditions.
