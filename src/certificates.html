Certificates & registrations

The registrations Geo Excel lists for food and leather trade. Copies can be requested with your enquiry.

  • APEDA

    Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority

    Registration with India's export promotion body for agricultural and processed food products.

  • FSSAI

    Food Safety and Standards Authority of India

    Food business licence/registration under India's food safety regulator.

  • IEC

    Importer-Exporter Code

    The business identification number required for import and export from India.

  • Udyam

    Udyam (MSME) Registration

    Registration as a micro, small or medium enterprise with the Government of India.

  • CLE

    Council for Leather Exports

    Membership of the export promotion council for leather and leather products.
